\section{Conclusion \& Reproducibility}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper contributes a characterisation and a specification. The
characterisation is TCF: three necessary conditions that together produce two
archives of one account, each internally complete, overlapping in part, and
irreconcilable through the platform that holds them. Bifurcation and independent
persistence are the conditions that create the split; the restore that replaces
rather than merges is the one that makes it permanent. The specification is CARTA:
\carta, with typed data passing between stages, records that cannot be safely
interpreted routed to a quarantine set with reason codes instead of being resolved
by guessing, and a reconstruction report carrying provenance, quarantine, conflicts
and counts as a first-class output.

Two of those elements are what the survey in Section~\ref{sec:related} could not
find anywhere among the neighbours. Every tool examined that merges chat archives
either discards input it cannot parse, prints a diagnostic and moves on, or aborts
the load, and none retains that input as an inspectable output. None emits a
structured account of what the reconstruction did. The forensic lineage supplies
staging and per-record provenance, both of which predate this work and neither of
which we claim; the reference super-timeline engine retains typed warnings but
keeps them out of the artefact an analyst reads. $\mathcal{Q}$ and $\Omega$ are the
two properties this specification adds to that inheritance.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
One artefact in the accompanying repository runs and reproduces: the synthetic
fixture generator. Running
\texttt{node scripts/generate-synthetic-fixtures.js -{}-seed 1 -{}-count 5}
regenerates the five committed fixtures and their manifest byte for byte, and we
verified this across consecutive runs and against the committed files. That result
covers deterministic generation of a synthetic corpus across 19 recorded
edge-case categories, and it covers nothing else. CHAMP-Pipeline is not
implemented, so no reconstruction, no unified archive, and no report can be
reproduced from this repository, and no measurement in this paper depends on any
that could be.

What remains is the part a specification cannot settle on its own. The strongest
claim available here is that a reconstruction which cannot say what it dropped is
indistinguishable, to the person reading it, from one that dropped nothing. Every
merging tool we examined leaves its reader in exactly that position. Whether
$\mathcal{Q}$ and $\Omega$ are the right instruments for closing that gap is a
question an implementation will answer better than an argument.
